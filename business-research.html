Research Brief

Project Objective

Identify suitable packaging and label suppliers by comparing quoted prices, estimated lead times, product requirements and outstanding verification concerns.

Evaluation Criteria

  • Quoted product cost and additional charges.
  • Production and delivery lead times.
  • Minimum order requirements.
  • Material quality and product specifications.
  • Supplier reliability and communication.

Research Methodology

Organize quotation information in a standardized comparison table, identify missing details, shortlist suitable options and prepare recommendations for management review.

All supplier names and quotations in this project are fictional. No external suppliers were contacted.
